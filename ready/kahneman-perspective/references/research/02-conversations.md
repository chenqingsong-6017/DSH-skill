# 02 · 对话现场：被追问时他怎么想

> 调研范围：Daniel Kahneman（1934–2024）的长访谈、播客、公开演讲逐字稿。
> 目标不是复述结论，而是记录**即兴推理的过程**：他先做什么、在哪一句改口、在哪一句收手。
>
> 标注约定：
> - 【原文】= 逐字稿原话（英文原句 + 我的直译）
> - 【转述】= 记者/主持人/书评人的话，不是他说的
> - 【我推断】= 我的解释，非引语
> - 【原文未核实】= 广为流传但我没拿到一手逐字稿

## 1. 被追问时的第一动作：不是辩护，是换坐标系

他的即兴反应有一个稳定顺序，在 CWT 里可以数出来：

**（1）先否认提问里偷渡的概念，再回答。**

Cowen 问"阿根廷盛行精神分析，作为心理学家你看这是 bias 吗"：

> 【原文】"You seem to attribute . . . You seem to think that I think of bias all the time. … It's like thinking of sex all the time. I really don't think of bias that much."
> （你似乎以为我整天想着 bias。……这就像整天想着性一样。我真没那么多地想 bias。）

这一句是全场最锋利的自我澄清：**他不接受"偏差研究者"这个身份框**。他不是在否认研究，是在否认"我只有一把锤子"的归因。

**（2）先给一个能当场算的数字，再给机制。**

谈 noise 时他没有先讲定义，而是先讲一个"我预期 10%，实测 50%"的实验：

> 【原文】"Suppose you take two people at random, two underwriters at random. You average the premium they set, you take the difference between them, and you divide the difference by the average. By what percentage do people differ? … It's somewhere around 10 percent. That's what people expect to see in a well-run firm. Now, what we found was 50 percent, 5–0, which, by the way, means that those underwriters were absolutely wasting their time, in the sense of assessing risk."
> （随便抽两个承保人，把两人定的保费之差除以均值，差异是百分之几？大家——包括公司高管——都说大约 10%。我们测出来是 50%。这意味着那些承保人在评估风险这件事上，完全在浪费时间。）

注意顺序：**提问 → 你先猜 → 我给出你的猜测 → 我给出实测 → 我才说这意味着什么**。他先把听者放进"预期落空"的位置，再讲结论。这是他一贯的即兴修辞结构，不是先讲理论。

**（3）把"规范性"从"描述性"里切出去。**

被问"知道 bias 也改不了吗"，他不给励志答案，也不给虚无答案：

> 【原文】"You can talk yourself out of some biases, I think. I wouldn't generalize on that, but it would take . . . I could possibly talk myself out of that one by really repeating to myself how stupid it is. But it would take a lot of work. It's not that you can decide once and for all, 'I will not be subject to that bias.' Doesn't work that way."
> （有些偏差你能说服自己摆脱，但我不做推广。我可以靠反复跟自己说这有多蠢来摆脱那一个，但那要花很大力气。不是"我一次性决定不再受这个偏差影响"就行的。不是那样运作的。）

**"一次决定" vs "持续做功"**——这是他区分描述与规范时最常用的一刀。

**（4）直接用"我 pass"结束，不补充体面话。**

被问"有什么值得乐观的"：

> 【原文】"I'm going to pass."（这题我过。）

被问"高中生该怎么用你的研究选大学"：

> 【原文】"My research adds absolutely nothing to this. There are sensible ways of choosing colleges, and I think they're well known… I have nothing to add, I think."

被问"怎么降低政治极化"：

> 【原文】"It's not that I have an answer and I'm suppressing it. … I have no idea. I don't have an answer. … How polarization can be reduced is too big a problem for me and, I think, currently for behavioral economics."

【我推断】这三句合起来是一条硬边界：**他区分"我能给你一个更好的思考方式"与"我能给你一个答案"**。前者他肯给（divide and conquer、延迟直觉），后者他几乎从不给。这条边界是他可信度的来源，也是他反复被误解的地方。

## 2. 改变立场的明确瞬间

这是本次调研最有价值的收获：**他改口时不说"我错了"，而说"我轻信了"（gullible）——把错误归因从"推理失败"改成"信任失败"。**

### 2.1 priming：唯一一次完整的公开改口（2021）

UNSW 访谈，被问到社会启动效应：

> 【原文】"It is true that when I wrote Thinking Fast and Slow, I was very impressed by literature on priming. And those were subtle, fascinating effects… And, and I think now that I was gullible, I think that I believed in these results, although if I had looked more carefully, I would have seen that the studies were individually fairly weak, and with small samples, the effects were too large to be true, in some sense. So yes, I became, and that really hurts me because I had put a lot of faith in it."
> （写《思考，快与慢》时我确实被 priming 文献打动了……我现在认为我当时很轻信。我信了这些结果，其实只要看得仔细一点，我本该看出单项研究都很弱、样本很小、效应大到不可能是真的。是的，我变了，这真的很伤我，因为我曾对它投入了很多信念。）

紧接着他把责任落到自己身上：

> 【原文】"I know because I did it myself, I caught myself in having made those errors."
> （我知道，因为我自己就这么干过，我抓到过自己犯这些错。）

并且拒绝把责任推给对手：

> 【原文】"The researchers in priming have never admitted that they were wrong."
> （priming 领域的研究者从没承认自己错了。）

**关键细节**：他强调 2012 年那封著名邮件"did not intend to be published"，而且"**I still believed in priming when I wrote that letter, I believed in it much more than I do now**"。也就是说他的立场是**两段式移动**：2012 年"我相信，但你们必须自证"→ 2021 年"我当时轻信了"。中间不是一次顿悟，是九年。

### 2.2 2012 那封信：他先保护的是人，不是理论

一手文本（Nature 刊出的邮件全文）里最不像"学术打假"的两句：

> 【原文】"My reason for writing this letter is that I see a train wreck looming. I expect the first victims to be young people on the job market."
> （我写这封信是因为我看见一场列车事故正在逼近。我预计第一批受害者是就业市场上的年轻人。）

> 【原文】"I am old enough to remember two fields that went into a prolonged eclipse after similar outsider attacks on the replicability of findings: subliminal perception and dissonance reduction."
> （我老到还记得两个领域在遭到类似的外部可复现性质疑后陷入长期日食：阈下知觉和认知失调。）

> 【原文】"Count me as a general believer."
> （算我一个总体上的相信者。）

【我推断】这封信的目标函数不是"求真"，是"保住一个领域和里面的人"。他在信里甚至替对方解释失败："Replication is intended as cloning but there are mutations and they can be lethal."（复现本意是克隆，但克隆会有突变，突变可能致命。）——**他先假定失败方无罪**。

后果他也承担了：他接受 Chronicle of Higher Education 电话采访时说，Nature 那篇报道的标题"totally inaccurate"，他因此收到大量愤怒邮件，"may lose some friendships"。【转述 + 短引，B 级】

### 2.3 System 1/2：承认内容错，保住框架

> 【原文】"There were whole sets of results that I published in Thinking, Fast and Slow that I wish I hadn't published because they're not reliable."
> （书里有一整批结果，我后悔发表了，因为它们不可靠。）

但他立刻划出框架的豁免区：

> 【原文】"The idea of two systems is really anchored in a basic sort of fact of experience, that the process by which you get 2 plus 2 is fundamentally different from the way that you get 17 by 24. … That's immune to replication."
> （两个系统的想法锚在一个经验事实里：2+2 和 17×24 的过程根本不同。……这一点不受可复现性影响。）

**这就是他的改口语法：把主张拆成"可被数据杀死的部分"和"不可被数据杀死的部分"，只交出前者。**

### 2.4 幸福研究：不是改口，是退出

> 【原文】"I was very interested in maximizing experience, but this doesn't seem to be what people want to do. They actually want to maximize their satisfaction with themselves and with their lives. And that leads in completely different directions than the maximization of happiness. … that's one of the reasons that I actually left the field of happiness."
> （我当年想最大化体验，但人们要的似乎不是这个。他们要最大化对自己、对自己人生的满意度。这跟最大化幸福指向完全不同的方向。……这也是我离开幸福研究的原因之一。）

注意这句的形状：**不是"我错了"，是"我问错了问题"**。他发现"人应该最大化体验"是个规范性前提，而人们实际不按它活，于是他退出，而不是修正数据。

### 2.5 对"我会怎么改主意"的回答（元层面）

> 【原文】"If I knew how I would change my mind, I would have changed my mind. My guess is that there will be completely different frameworks… I know that this is going to happen to everything that I believe. Give it a few decades — it's going to be irrelevant. I wish I could peer into the future and know what comes next, but I can't."

**这是全套材料里最像"方法论自画像"的一段**：他相信的不是自己的结论，是"结论会被取代"这件事本身。

## 3. 直觉信号：三段完整叙事（逐字，不节选）

### 3.1 以军面试系统：他先不信直觉，后被自己的数据推翻（2021，UNSW）

这是他一生反复回讲的"原点故事"，UNSW 版最完整：

> 【原文】"I was assigned the task of setting up an interview system for combat recruits… there was an interview system in existence, which was the standard unstructured interview where the interviewer spoke to an individual and tried to form a general impression, and eventually got the sense that he or she knew the individual and could tell how good a soldier that individual would be. And that actually had very low validity. **So people had high confidence in their intuitions, and they were essentially useless.** And this is very common. That is, it's well known that an unstructured interview produces a lot of confidence in the interviewer, and very poor validity on average."

> 【原文】"The interviewers, who were on the job… were furious with me. And they were furious because they wanted that sense of intuition, they wanted to… use their clinical ability. **And I remember being told by one of them, you're turning us into robots.**"

> 【原文】"And so I compromised… you get those six scores. **But once you have completed the six scores, close your eyes, and make a judgement and intuitive judgement, how good a soldier would that person be?**"

> 【原文】"Now, a few months later, we had the results of that study… **But what was surprising to me then, was that the final intuitive judgement, close your eyes and make a judgement, was highly valid, and it added content, it added validity beyond the six traits that have been evaluated.**"

> 【原文】"**So now, the lesson I drew from that was that intuition does work, but you want to resist forming an intuition too quickly**, you want to resist, in the more recent terms, you want to resist fast thinking. You want to collect the information, you want to develop a profile of the case, and then you can allow yourself an intuitive judgement."

**这段的结构值得单独拆开**（这是本路最该复用的模板）：

1. **触发线索**：一个已被接受的流程「效度极低」，而使用者「信心极高」——**信心与效度的脱钩**是他所有直觉怀疑的母题。
2. **他的预期**：把判断拆成六个特质分，取平均，机械合成即可。
3. **他实际在保的目标**：不是"证明直觉坏"，而是**让系统能被接受**——所以他做了妥协（保留闭眼直觉这一栏）。这个妥协后来救了他：正因为保留了直觉栏，他才有机会发现直觉"adds validity"。
4. **结果反转**：直觉那一栏不但有效，还提供了六项特质之外的信息。
5. **他改的不是结论，是时序**：不是"信直觉/不信直觉"，是"**把直觉推到信息收集之后**"。

**这是"改变立场"最精确的样本：他没有从 A 翻到非 A，他把 A 挪到了时间轴的另一端。** 晚年他把这套东西回收成《Noise》里的"中介评估法（mediating assessment protocol）"，他自己点明："it was personally quite satisfying to go back to an idea that I had developed 60 years earlier and use it again."

### 3.2 承保人实验：他的直觉信号是"这个分布不可能"

> 【原文】"I asked the executives in the firm, and it's a number that just about everybody agrees… It's somewhere around 10 percent. That's what people expect to see in a well-run firm. Now, what we found was 50 percent."

【我推断】触发他的不是"数据难看"，是**"一家管理良好的公司不该出现这种离散度"这个先验**。他的直觉在这里是**先验与数据的冲突报警器**，不是判断工具。这跟他"我不相信自己的直觉"并不矛盾：他信的是"我对'不该是这样'的嗅觉"，不信的是"我对'就是这样'的断定"。

### 3.3 反向案例：他在 priming 上没有触发直觉信号（他自己承认）

这是最该记住的一段——**同一个人，在同一类判断上，信号没响**：

> 【原文】"if I had looked more carefully, I would have seen that the studies were individually fairly weak, and with small samples, the effects were too large to be true, in some sense."

他给出的漏检原因是**社会性的，不是认知性的**：

> 【原文】"I just assumed that, since they had survived the peer-review process, I have to accept them as scientific."【转述，Russ Roberts 在 EconTalk 引述 Kahneman 2011 年前后的博客回应；原文未核实】
> （我当时就假定：既然它们通过了同行评审，我就必须把它们当作科学来接受。）

【我推断】这条与 3.1 对照，能推出一个可操作的结论：**他的直觉警报只在"有外部基准（先验/分布/效度研究）"时响；在没有基准、只有权威背书的领域，他会静默通过。** 这是他自己给出的失败模式，比任何"他很有洞察力"的赞美都有用。

### 3.4 「我知道但我照犯」的三处自陈

> 【原文】"I'd rather be two hours late."（谈误机：明知两小时与一分钟结果相同，他仍宁愿晚两小时——近误效应照旧。）

> 【原文】"Despite 45 years of work in the field, I am still inclined to make over-confident predictions."（Guardian 2011，B 级）

> 【原文】"I never felt I was studying the stupidity of mankind in the third person. I always felt I was studying my own mistakes."（Guardian 2011，B 级）

第三句是他全部研究的第一人称定位。**他把自己放在样本里，而不是站在样本外。**

## 4. 新手错：「外行以为 X，其实 Y」

| 外行预设 | 他的纠正 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 偏差研究就是研究人有多蠢 | "I never felt I was studying the stupidity of mankind in the third person." | Guardian 2011 |
| 系统 1 / 系统 2 是大脑里的两块区域 | "the systems don't really exist. It's a very convenient way of talking about them"；且他**故意**违反"不许用小人解释人"的学科禁忌："it's a no no, that I very deliberately violated" | NPR 2011 / UNSW 2021 |
| 知道偏差就能避免偏差 | "It's not that you can decide once and for all… Doesn't work that way." | CWT 2018 |
| 减少误差 = 减少偏差 | "noise and bias are independent sources of error… the procedures by which you would reduce bias and reduce noise are not the same" | CWT 2018 |
| 群体智慧总能救场 | "The wisdom of the crowds will work… when errors are independent"；若大家共享同一偏差，讨论只会提高信心 | CWT 2018 |
| 人可以凭"感觉机器错了"推翻算法 | "when people override formulas, by and large, they do worse than if they hadn't" | CWT 2018 |
| 专家的直觉都值得听 | 三条件：**规律的世界 + 长期经验 + 快速明确的反馈**；CEO 大多不满足 | CWT 2018 |
| 行为经济学能解决大问题 | "behavioural economics is not going to solve climate change"；它只"at the margin"有效 | UNSW 2021 |
| 乐观是缺陷 | 个体层面期望值可能为负，社会层面它是资本主义的引擎（乐观与过度自信是两件事） | CWT 2018（综合转述 + 原句） |
| 讲座/畅销书能改变人的思维 | "I think you're giving the book much too credit. I don't think it has any influence about how people think, good or bad." | UNSW 2021 |

## 5. 他自陈的思维习惯与缺陷

**（a）不消费自己的记忆。**
> 【原文】"I do not consume my memories a lot. And I almost never go back to photographs, not deliberately. If I stumble on something, it will move me. But the idea of going back to relive a vacation — that's not what I do, so I have little empathy for this."

**（b）写作极慢。**
> 【原文】"He writes very easily, and I write with difficulty, so it's not an accident that we teamed up."（说 Cass Sunstein，反照自己）

**（c）当场健忘，且提前打招呼。**
> 【原文】"I will probably forget it by the time I finish answering one question, but you'll have to forgive me for that."

**（d）对数字有怪癖式的记忆，但否认这是天赋。**
> 【原文】"like 24 times 17… I think the answer is 408. … I happen to remember it. But, and that is work."

**（e）讨厌"功能主义"式的顺手解释。**
> 【原文】"I don't like those kinds of explanations. They look facile to me."（回应"以色列人说话直是因为常年危机"）

**（f）用词自我审查。**
> 【原文】"I very rarely use the word rational. And I never use the word irrational."——因为"rationality as defined is completely impractical… the question of whether people are rational or not is, in some sense, not even an interesting question."

**（g）归功于外部的习惯（可能也是防御）。**
> 【原文】"luck must be involved"；"I got too much credit for what he did."（说 Thaler）；"And no thanks to my letter."（说心理学改革）

**（h）极端不喜欢冲突——这条只有间接证据。** 2012 年信引发的风波里，他主动向同行解释"我不是在攻击你们"，并说"I wanted to give that advice because I felt for them"。【转述，B 级】他处理冲突的方式是**先声明善意，再给硬话**，而不是正面冲撞。

**（i）他对"改变行为"的悲观是彻底的。**
> 【原文】"anybody who's very optimistic about changing behavior is just deluded. It's hard to change other people's behavior. It's very hard to change your own."（KP #68）

**（j）他认为人的信念来源不是推理。**
> 【原文】"We have beliefs because mostly we believe in some people, and we trust them. We adopt their beliefs. We don't reach our beliefs by clear thinking, unless you're a scientist or doing something like that."（KP #68）

## 7. 关于 Amos Tversky：可核实的只有这些

必须坦白：**「我们像一个人」「轮流起草」「谁说最后一句」这些具体说法，我在任何一手逐字稿里都没找到。**

可核实的：

> 【原文】"the mental energy, just the *joy* of thinking… our joint work had a touch of irony to it. The fact that we were laughing continuously as we were doing the work was very important to the nature of what we did."（CWT 2018）

> 【原文】"we were examining our own thinking, and finding stupid things in our own thinking, and finding that delightful and very funny."（CWT 2018）

> 【原文】"Amos Tversky, my research partner, and I were better together than on our own. We sort of knew that. Mostly it was extremely pleasant not trying to work everything out yourself."（Guardian 2011）

> 【原文】Kahneman 2003, *American Psychologist*（经 Thagard 2006 引用）："The experience was magical… In his presence, I became funny as well, and the result was that we could spend hours of solid work in continuous mirth."

【转述】NYT 书评（2016）引 Michael Lewis 书："they would sit next to each other at a single typewriter. **'We were sharing a mind,'** one would say later. They flipped a coin to decide whose name would appear first."
——**"sharing a mind" 是转述中的引语，说话人未标明，我不把它当作 Kahneman 原话使用。**

【我推断】他谈 Amos 时的语气与谈研究时明显不同：句子变短、形容词变多、几乎不给机制解释。这是他少数**不分析**的话题。

## 8. TED 2010：记忆自我的三句硬话

（B 级，多源一致的短引）

> "We actually don't choose between experiences, we choose between memories of experiences."
> "the remembering self is a storyteller… What defines a story are changes, significant moments and endings. Endings are very, very important."
> "the tyranny of the remembering self… dragging the experiencing self through a set of experiences it doesn't need."

结肠镜实验的**结论**可靠：两位病人，A 的疼痛峰值较低、结束时已无痛；B 峰值更高、持续时间更长、但结束时仍在痛——**B 回忆中的痛苦反而更轻**。这支撑"峰终定律 + 时长忽视"。

## 10. 可直接复用的三条行为规则（从他身上提炼，非他的原话）

1. **先让提问者下注，再给数据。**（"你觉得是多少？""大约 10%。""实测 50%。"）
2. **改口时改归因，不改人格。** 不说"我判断错了"，说"我轻信了权威"——前者否定能力，后者定位流程漏洞。
3. **把直觉从"第一步"挪到"最后一步"，而不是删掉它。** 这是他从 1950 年代以军面试到 2021 年《Noise》唯一没变过的方法论。
