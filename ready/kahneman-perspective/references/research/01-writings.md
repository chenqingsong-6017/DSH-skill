## 2. 他说过的（一手）

### 2.0 核心论文清单与可核验断言

| 论文 | 出处 | 可核验断言（原文措辞） |
|---|---|---|
| 〈Belief in the Law of Small Numbers〉 | Tversky & Kahneman, *Psychological Bulletin* 76:105, 1971 | 科学家错误相信小样本能复制总体的"大数定律"（B，经 1974 参考文献与自传互证） |
| 〈Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases〉 | *Science* 185(4157):1124–1131, 1974 | 三条启发式：representativeness / availability / adjustment from an anchor（A） |
| 〈Prospect Theory〉 | *Econometrica* 47(2):263–291, 1979 | certainty effect、isolation effect、价值函数对收益凹对损失凸且损失更陡、决策权重（A） |
| 〈Choices, Values, and Frames〉 | *American Psychologist* 39(4):341–350, 1984 | 亚洲疾病问题、不变性失效、心理账户（A，全文可得） |
| 〈Maps of Bounded Rationality〉（诺奖讲座） | *AER* 93(5):1449–1475, 2003 | 明确把研究定位为"给有限理性画地图"，且"primarily a contribution to psychology"（A） |
| 〈A Perspective on Judgment and Choice〉 | *American Psychologist* 58(9):697–720, 2003 | 首次系统提出 adversarial collaboration，p.729（B） |
| 〈Living, and thinking about it〉 | 收于 Huppert et al. 编 *The Science of Well-being*, OUP, pp.285–304, 2005 | 两个自我、peak/end rule、duration neglect（A，全文可得） |
| 〈Would You Be Happier If You Were Richer? A Focusing Illusion〉 | *Science* 312(5782):1908–1910, 2006 | 收入对"生活满意度"有效、对"当下情绪"几乎无效（A） |
| 《Noise》 | 2021 | 误差 = 偏差 + 噪声；噪声分 level / pattern / occasion 三层（B） |

**四篇论文的原文关键句（A）**

1974：三条启发式**不是缺陷，而是省力的正确策略**——
> "These heuristics are highly economical and usually effective, but they lead to systematic and predictable errors."

1979：全篇的立论两句（逐字，见摘要）——
> "an alternative theory of choice is developed, in which value is assigned to gains and losses rather than to final assets and in which probabilities are replaced by decision weights."
> "The value function is normally concave for gains, commonly convex for losses, and is generally steeper for losses than for gains."

1984：亚洲疾病实验的硬数据（他在世时最常被引的实验）——问题 1（N=152）：确定救 200 人获 72%，赌一把获 28%；问题 2（N=155）同一情境改成"确定死 400 人"：确定方案只剩 22%，赌一把升至 78%。他的判词：
> "The failure of invariance is both pervasive and robust."
> "In their stubborn appeal, framing effects resemble perceptual illusions more than computational errors."

2005：以"刮花的唱片毁掉整场交响乐"为例，指出被毁的不是体验而是记忆——
> "the experience was not ruined, only the memory of it … The confusion of experience with memory that makes us believe a past experience can be ruined is a compelling cognitive illusion."（A, p.286）

**文本谱系（用于理解他如何把论文长成书）**
1974 Science 三条启发式 → 1982 合集 → 2002 合集 → 2011《Thinking, Fast and Slow》前四部分；
1999《Well-being》合集 + 2005 章节 + 2006 Science → 2011 该书第五部分"两个自我"；
2016 HBR〈Noise〉→ 2021《Noise》。
即：**他几乎不写"新书"，而是把二十年论文按教学顺序重新编排**。这解释了他著作中大量重复案例（琳达问题、亚洲疾病、冷水实验）——重复不是懒惰，是他确认命题稳定性的方式。

### 2.1 信念形成故事（"我信 X 因为我经历了 Y"）

**① 幻觉效度：在以色列军队评估新兵时第一次撞见"认知幻觉"**（A，诺奖自传 "The military experience" 节）
> "I was so impressed by the complete lack of connection between the statistical information and the compelling experience of insight that I coined a term for it: 'the illusion of validity.' … It was the first cognitive illusion I discovered."

关键补充：他不是先有理论再找证据，而是先被**统计预测与实际表现零相关**这件事"震住"，才发明了词。同一节交代了方法论来源：
> "the idea that predictions should be less extreme than the information on which they are based is deeply counterintuitive."

**② 飞行教官与回归均值：他自认一生中最重要的"喜悦时刻"**（A，同上 "Training to become a professional" 节）
> "This was a joyous moment, in which I understood an important truth about the world: because we tend to reward others when they do well and punish them when they do badly, and because there is regression to the mean, it is part of the human condition that we are statistically punished for rewarding others and rewarded for punishing them."

紧接着的一句，是整个认知框架的种子——**他知道证据改不了信念**：
> "But I knew that this demonstration would not undo the effects of lifelong exposure to a perverse contingency."

**③ 统计性思维是被"教"出来的，不是天生的**（A，同上）
> "To teach effectively I did a lot of serious thinking about valid intuitions on which I could draw and erroneous intuitions that I should teach students to overcome. I had no idea, of course, but I was laying the foundation for a program of research on judgment under uncertainty."

**④ Meehl 的书给了他"临床直觉不如简单公式"的实证底座**（A，同上）
> "The most instructive finding was that the interviewers' global evaluation, produced at the end of a structured interview, was by far the most predictive of all the ratings they made. Trying to be reliable had made them valid."

**⑤ 早年对自己的可复制性要求近乎苛刻——后来却破了戒**（A，同上，第 3 段）
> "in order not to pollute the literature, I wanted to report only findings that I had replicated in detail at least once … I realized only gradually that my aspirations demanded more statistical power and therefore much larger samples than I was intuitively inclined to run."

**⑥ 与 Amos Tversky 的合作如何开始**（A，同上 "The collaboration with Amos Tversky" 节）
起因是 1968–69 年他开的研究生讨论课，请年轻同事 Tversky 来讲判断与决策；两人午饭时交换"自己反复犯的判断错误"的私人账本，决定研究专家的统计直觉。他给这段关系的定性：
> "Amos and I shared the wonder of together owning a goose that could lay golden eggs – a joint mind that was better than our separate minds."

以及一条极少被引、但极具操作性的边界条件：
> "Our magic worked only when we were by ourselves. We soon learned that joint collaboration with any third party should be avoided, because we became competitive in a threesome."

**⑦ 智识谱系（他自己点名的）**
- Herbert Simon：A，2003 AER 诺奖讲座摘要开篇即写 "Herbert A. Simon (1955, 1979) had proposed much earlier that decision makers should be viewed as boundedly rational, and had offered a model in which utility maximization was replaced by satisficing."（A）
- Richard Thaler：A，自传 "Behavioral economics" 节，把整合功劳明确让给 Thaler——"in my view the work of integration was actually done mostly by Thaler and the group of young economists that quickly began to form around him"。
- Anne Treisman（妻子）：A，自传仅 5 处提及，最具体的是 1965–66 年在哈佛听到她的注意研究演讲，"who would become my wife twelve years later"，后期与她合作视觉注意与客体知觉研究。**注意：他把"注意"这条线与"判断偏差"线并行发展，二者在 System 1/System 2 里合流——这是他思想史的关键接缝。**
- Meehl (1954)：A，见 ④。
- Rapaport：A，"I realized only while writing the acknowledgments for the book that I had revisited the terrain to which Rapaport had first led me."

### 2.2 真实决策复盘

**① 故意给理论起一个"无意义的名字"**（A，自传 "Prospect theory" 节）
> "when we were ready to submit the work for publication, we deliberately chose a meaningless name for our theory: 'prospect theory.' We reasoned that if the theory ever became well known, having a distinctive label would be an advantage."

**② 防御性写作：先假想一个挑刺的研究生**（A，同上）
> "we invented the specter of an ambitious graduate student looking for flaws, and we labored to make that student's task as thankless as possible."

**③ 投稿选择本身就是一次"运气决策"**（A，2002 诺奖访谈）
> "if we'd published that word for word in Psychological Review … there would have been no Nobel Prize for this work today. So it was because it happened to be published in Econometrica that this happened."

同年访谈的收尾句，是他对"迟来的理解"的注解：
> "It takes a long time to understand what you've been saying."（A）

**④ 对抗性合作（adversarial collaboration）的发明过程**（A，Edge 演讲〈Adversarial Collaboration〉）
起点是一次他和 Gilovich/Medvec 关于"后悔"的分歧：
> "We ran an experiment, and everyone was wrong. It turned out that delayed regret is mostly wistful, but it can be intense."
制度动机：
> "Adversarial collaboration is an alternative to what I call 'angry science.'" / "Doing angry science is a demeaning experience."
正式定义（A，Kahneman 2003, *American Psychologist*, 58(9), p.729，经二手转引但页码一致）：
> "a good-faith effort to conduct debates by carrying out joint research—in some cases, an agreed-upon arbiter may be needed to lead the project and collect the data."

**⑤ 与 Gigerenzer 阵营长达 37 年的对立，他选择"不回应敌意论文"**（A，Edge）
> "Gerd Gigerenzer published his first critique of our work thirty-seven years ago, and he's still not done with me."
> "my phobia for professional anger is such that I have allowed myself for many years the luxury of refusing to referee papers that might arouse that emotion"（A，自传）

### 2.3 本人承认的失败与错误

**① 2011 年"priming 不可不信"→ 2017 年公开认错**（A，对 Replicability-Index 博客的公开回应，2017-02-14；亦见 RetractionWatch 2017-02-20 转载）
> "I placed too much faith in underpowered studies."
> "there is a special irony in my mistake because the first paper that Amos Tversky and I published was about the belief in the 'law of small numbers,' which allows researchers to trust the results of underpowered studies with unreasonably small samples."
> "However, I now understand that my reasoning was flawed and that I should have known better."
> "I have changed my views about the size of behavioral priming effects – they cannot be as large and as robust as my chapter suggested."（A，转引 mindhacks 2017-02-16 引其原话）

被推翻的原话（B，《Thinking, Fast and Slow》第 4 章）：
> "disbelief is not an option. The results are not made up, nor are they statistical flukes. You have no choice but to accept that the major conclusions of these studies are true."

**② 他自己认定的"揭示性错误"：一辈子没把 representativeness 讲出好处**（A，自传）
> "My long-standing failure to associate specific benefits to the concept of representativeness was a revealing mistake."

**③ 承认早年在"利用随机"**（A，自传 "The military experience"）
> "I was capitalizing on chance, but the technique had enough charm for one of my graduate-school teachers … to write it up in what became my first published article."

**④ 承认博士论文很糟**（A，自传）
> "so convoluted and dreary that my teacher, Susan Ervin, memorably described the experience of reading it as 'wading through wet mush.'"

**⑤ 承认被 Guth 抢先（最后通牒博弈）**（A，自传）
> "I remember being quite crestfallen when I learned this."

**⑥ 承认误判了与 Amos 合作衰退的机制**（A，自传）
> "We had completely failed to appreciate how critically our successful interaction had depended on our being together at the birth of every significant idea … We struggled for years to revive the magic we had lost, but in vain."

**⑦ 承认"均衡"这个概念当年不在他的工具箱里**（A，自传 "Behavioral economics"）
> "It took me some time to realize that the magic we were observing was an equilibrium … This idea had not been in my intellectual bag of tools."

**⑧ 明确改口"生活满意度"的性质**（B，TFAS 第 37 章 p.397）
> "Life satisfaction is not a flawed measure of their experienced well-being, as I thought some years ago. It is something else entirely."

### 2.4 反复出现 ≥3 次的核心命题（真信念）

**命题 A：主观确信度不能作为判断正确性的证据。**
出现：1974 Science（启发式"highly economical and usually effective"却产生"systematic and predictable errors"）→ 自传"illusion of validity" → TFAS 第 20 章 p.212 → 2011 NYT〈Don't Blink〉。
最锋利的一句（B，TFAS p.212）：
> "declarations of high confidence mainly tell you that an individual has constructed a coherent story in his mind, not necessarily that the story is true."

**命题 B：统计性思维与直觉不相容；直觉在统计维度上系统性失明。**
出现：1971〈Belief in the Law of Small Numbers〉→ 自传飞行教官 → TFAS 第 17 章 p.181："whenever the correlation between two scores is imperfect, there will be regression to the mean." → 1974 Science。
自传那句"deeply counterintuitive"是本命题的原点。

**命题 C：故事靠连贯性取胜，不靠完整性（WYSIATI 家族）。**
出现：1974 representativeness → TFAS 第 7 章 p.87 → 第 19 章 p.201 → 第 20 章 p.212。
> "It is the consistency of the information that matters for a good story, not its completeness."（B, p.87）
> "Our comforting conviction that the world makes sense rests on a secure foundation: our almost unlimited ability to ignore our ignorance."（B, p.201）

**命题 D：体验自我与记忆自我是两个不同的主体，而做决定的是记忆自我。**
出现：2005〈Living, and thinking about it〉→ 2006 Science〈Would You Be Happier…〉→ TFAS 第 35–38 章 → 2011 Bloomberg。
2005 原文（A，pp.285–286）：
> "with very few exceptions, they simply disappear. The experiencing self that lives each of these moments barely has time to exist."
> "The remembering self is sometimes simply wrong."（A, p.286）
制度含义（A, p.286）："It is a basic fact of the human condition that memories are what we get to keep from our experience."

**命题 E：运气在成功叙事中的权重被系统性低估。**
出现：TFAS 引言 p.9 → 2002 诺奖访谈（Econometrica 之幸）→ 自传（投硬币定作者顺序、被 Guth 抢先）。
> "luck plays a large role in every story of success; it is almost always easy to identify a small change in the story that would have turned a remarkable achievement into a mediocre outcome."（B, p.9）

**命题 F：误差 = 偏差 + 噪声；噪声是可测量、可审计的系统性现象，不是"人各有见"的多样性。**
出现：2016 HBR〈Noise〉→ 2021《Noise》→ 2021 哈佛访谈。
《Noise》全书反复句（C，经维基百科条目转述为书中反复出现）："wherever there is judgment, there is noise, and more of it than you think."
> "Noise is inherently statistical: it becomes visible only when we think statistically about an ensemble of similar judgments."（C，转引，未核页码）`[页码未核实]`

**命题 G：证据消失了，信念还站着（信念耐力）。**
出现：自传（"I knew that this demonstration would not undo the effects of lifelong exposure to a perverse contingency"）→ Edge 演讲 → 2017 认错文（承认改变的是他对自己推理的评价，而非对 priming 的整体立场）。
> "The evidence is gone, but the beliefs are still standing. Indeed, I cannot think of a single important opinion that I have changed as a result of losing my faith in the studies of behavioral priming, although they seemed quite important to me at the time."（A，Edge）

**方法论底色（贯穿全部著作）**：
> "I have always wanted to do more than could be done without risk of error, and have always taken pride in preferring to be approximately right rather than precisely wrong. Amos thought that if you pick the terrain properly you won't have to choose, because you can be precisely right."（A，自传·悼词）

## 3. 别人说他的（二手，C）

- **System 1 / System 2 缺乏实证支持**：Melnikoff & Bargh (2018), *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 22(4):280–293，〈The Mythical Number Two〉，称二分法是 "a convenient and seductive myth"，理由：多数心理过程混合两类特征、框架不可证伪、且以"结果对错"反推系统归属属结果偏倚。https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/abstract/S1364-6613(18)30024-X
- **priming 章节不可复制**：Schimmack, Heene & Kesavan (2017-02-02), 〈Reconstruction of a Train Wreck〉，计算 TFAS 第 4 章所引 12 篇文献的 R-Index，称 11/12 低于 50，31 项研究 100% 显著、中位观测功效 57%。https://replicationindex.com/2017/02/02/reconstruction-of-a-train-wreck-how-priming-research-went-of-the-rails/
- **"对抗性合作"概念归属**：Tetlock 评价"他会很快说自己不是发明者，但短语确实是他造的"（Penn Today）。https://penntoday.upenn.edu/news/pursuit-scientific-truth-adversarial-collaboration-Tetlock-Clark
- **Thaler 对他的评价**：Kahneman 称 Thaler 的 "Anomalies" 专栏 "nothing short of brilliant, funny and were always in some way devastating to a piece of theory"（UBS Nobel Perspectives 引）。此为他对别人的评价，非别人对他。

## 4. 我推断的（D，仅供建模，不可当引语）

1. **他的思考方式是"先造测量工具，再谈理论"**：从军队结构化面试、单问题问卷、日重建法（DRM）、噪声审计，一路都是"把一个模糊概念压成一次可操作的测量"。这比"双系统"更是他的方法内核。
2. **他对"自己是否被说服"极度不信任**，因此把"改变想法"外化成制度（对抗性合作、指定仲裁人、预注册式约定），而非依靠个人诚实。这与自传"我在论战中很少记得被说服"互为因果。
3. **他对"叙事诱惑"的警惕是针对自己的**：他反复写"连贯性骗人"，而他 2011 年正是被"多实验室结果一致"说服。他的核心命题大概率源自对自己的长期观察，而非对普通人的俯视。
4. **"两个自我"是他唯一带有价值判断的领域**：他多次暗示政策应更看重体验自我，这与他其余部分"只描述不规训"的姿态不一致（详见矛盾 5）。

## 5. 矛盾清单（双方并存，不调和）

1. **小样本**：1971 年论文主题就是"人们错误相信小样本能代表总体"；2011 年他却信任一批 n≈30 的 priming 研究。他自己称之为 "special irony"。
2. **立场三次移动**：2011 "disbelief is not an option" → 2012 公开信 "I see a train wreck looming" → 2017 "my reasoning was flawed"。三次都不是被新数据推动，而是被元科学（功效分析、R-Index）推动。
3. **"我很少被论战说服" vs 2017 公开认错**：他一边写 "I have few memories of having changed my mind under adversarial pressure"，一边在博客评论区（非同行评审、非对抗性合作程序）认了错。他承认的"改变"恰恰发生在他自己设计的制度之外。
4. **厌恶争论 vs 终身论战**：自传称"拒绝评审可能引发怒气的稿件"，同时与 Gigerenzer 阵营对峙 37 年，并主动发明对抗性合作程序。
5. **描述 vs 规范**：多数著作坚持"这是描述，不是说不理性"（"I am now quick to reject any description of our work as demonstrating human irrationality"），但"两个自我"与"噪声审计"章节明显带有"应当如何决策"的规范主张。
6. **他自己承认表述曾经不严谨**："Was I always so careful? Probably not."（自传）——这句话是理解他所有早期文本的免责声明。
