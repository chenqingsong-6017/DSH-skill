## 流水与数字

55. "It's somewhere around 10 percent. That's what people expect to see in a well-run firm. Now, what we found was 50 percent, 5–0, which, by the way, means that those underwriters were absolutely wasting their time, in the sense of assessing risk."——CWT 2018。A
55b. 「2015 年，我们对一家大型保险公司的核保员做了研究。48 名核保员看到同一批风险摘要并定价……两名核保员之间的典型差异达到其平均保费的 55%。」——《Noise》作者 NYT 客座文（2021-05-15），英文原文："In 2015, we conducted a study of underwriters in a large insurance company.
